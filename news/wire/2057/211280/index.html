Whitman Signs 5-Year Extension with Boston

November 30, 2057 · From the league's news service

The Boston Red Sox have finished a deal with All Star-caliber right fielder Dan Whitman that will extend his stay in Boston at least 5 years and will pay out $115,000,000.

Whitman has a career .229 average with 1017 hits, including 221 doubles, 36 triples and 231 homers, collected 616 RBIs, scored 850 runs and compiled a .374 OBP.
