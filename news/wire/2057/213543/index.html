SP DuBay, Chicago (N) Reach Accord

January 20, 2058 · From the league's news service

Chicago (N) came to terms with veteran starting pitcher Ryan DuBay today, locking up the 35-year-old to a $7,500,000, 1-year deal.

In his career, DuBay has crafted a 25-31 record with a 4.08 ERA in 575.2 innings. Opposing clubs are batting .237 against him and he has rung up 554 strikeouts.
