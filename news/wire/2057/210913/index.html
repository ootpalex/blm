Best AL Reliever Named

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

They say you can't teach an old dog new tricks, but in the case of Brandon Robison of the Kansas City Royals, you don't have to - his old tricks worked just fine in 2057.

The 34-year-old veteran turned in a sparkling performance and has won the American League Reliever of the Year.

He captured the award with 46 saves in 64 relief appearances, 88 strikeouts and 17 walks in 60.2 innings. He put up a record of 5-3 to go with his 1.34 ERA.

Chris Willard of the Seattle Mariners finished second in voting, while Dan Patterson of the Chicago (A) White Sox finished third.
