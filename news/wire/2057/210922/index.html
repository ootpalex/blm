NL Names Silver Slugger Award Winners

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

A Silver Slugger Award honors "the pinnacle of offensive performance at a respective position," the Major League Baseball commissioner stated. The National League winners for 2057 are:

Catcher
Dylon MacDonald (Miami Marlins)
.263/.325/.457, 460 AB, 21 HR, 1 SB, 4.5 WAR

First Baseman
Alex Lobato (San Francisco Giants)
.323/.392/.511, 613 AB, 13 HR, 18 SB, 7.4 WAR

Remo Carone (Los Angeles (N) Dodgers)
.247/.364/.455, 486 AB, 22 HR, 42 SB, 5.5 WAR
