$11,220,000 Lands RP Galeazzi for Giants

December 31, 2057 · From the league's news service

San Francisco's signing of David Galeazzi was met with mixed reactions today as the 29-year-old reliever was introduced to the media. Some experts consider him overpaid at $3,740,000 a year for 3 years, while others insist that Galeazzi is a perfect fit for the Giants.

Galeazzi has 35 wins and 25 losses with 89 saves in his career. His ERA stands at 3.14, and he has 353 strikeouts in 418 innings.
