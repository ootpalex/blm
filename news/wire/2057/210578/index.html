Lobato Remains with San Francisco

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

Team officials triumphantly announced that the Giants and superstar first baseman Alex Lobato have closed a deal that keeps the 31-year-old in a San Francisco uniform for another 6 years.

Lobato will earn $299,200,000 as part of the settlement.

Lobato has batted .320 for his career with 1876 hits, 414 doubles, 72 triples, 120 home runs, and 776 RBIs.
