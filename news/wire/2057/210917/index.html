NL Awards Most Valuable Player Award, Titterington Gets Prize

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

He may only be 24, but it will be hard to top the shortstop's 2057 campaign, as he has been named the winner of the National League Most Valuable Player Award.

His winning campaign featured a .318 batting average and a .379 on-base percentage this year. In 148 games he piled up 177 hits, 29 doubles, 5 triples, 37 home runs, 99 RBIs and 92 runs scored.

Angel Moya of the Los Angeles (N) Dodgers finished second in voting, while Alex Lobato of the San Francisco Giants finished third.
