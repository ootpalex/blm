Cordova Commits to $13,360,000 Deal with Washington

February 20, 2058 · From the league's news service

In a move expected to bring depth to the Nationals, Washington brass announced the signing today of reliever Joel Cordova.

Cordova will be pocketing $4,453,333 per season, an amount some BNN analysts think might be too high.

Cordova has 37 saves in his career with a 27-33 record and a 3.66 ERA.
