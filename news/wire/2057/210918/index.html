2057 NL Cy Young Award Chosen

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

Jorge Vasquez of the Washington Nationals might be an old man, but he can still pitch with the best of them. Today the 36-year-old hurler snagged the National League Cy Young Award honors for his outstanding 2057 performance.

He posted a 2.38 ERA with 17 wins, 5 losses, 226 strikeouts and 53 walks in 222.2 innings.

Nick Stuefen of the Milwaukee Brewers finished second in voting, while Clint Hernandez of the Washington Nationals finished third.
