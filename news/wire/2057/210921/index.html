NL Honors 2057 Gold Glove Award Winners

November 21, 2057 · From the league's news service

Defense is a critical element in the game of baseball. Many times late in the game, a light-hitting defensive specialist will be inserted in place of a superstar hitter. Good defenders are worth their weight in gold and today the best of them for 2057 have been recognized. These players will receive the National League Gold Glove Award:

Pitcher
Ivan Colindres (St. Louis Cardinals)

Catcher
Brian Jones (New York (N) Mets)

First Baseman
Carmen Johnson (Los Angeles (N) Dodgers)

Second Baseman
Danny Pollard (Milwaukee Brewers)

Third Baseman
Cruz Maltez (Chicago (N) Cubs)

Shortstop
Jeff Redpath (Cincinnati Reds)

Left Fielder
Matt Rosellini (San Diego Padres)

Center Fielder
Jake Hite (Milwaukee Brewers)

Right Fielder
Ben Reed (Arizona Diamondbacks)
