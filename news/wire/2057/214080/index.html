Baltimore Commits $168,000,000 to 3B McIver

February 3, 2058 · From the league's news service

Rumors of a deal between Marty McIver and Baltimore finally bore fruit today as the two sides faced a packed press corps. Orioles officials looked very pleased as they outlined the terms of the new deal.

McIver will earn $33,600,000 per year for 5 years. He is expected to be a solid addition to his new club.

Lifetime McIver has played in 901 games, batted .297 with 81 home runs and 1008 base hits.
