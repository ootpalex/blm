Gomez, Cincinnati Finalize $36,000,000 Contract

February 19, 2058 · From the league's news service

In what local scribes are hailing as a huge move for the team's future, the Reds inked a $36,000,000 deal with starting pitcher Leo Gomez.

Team officials expect Gomez to fill an immediate void on the roster and felt the deal would solidify them as a playoff contender.

Career-to-date, Gomez has a record of 178 wins and 149 losses and a 3.90 ERA.
