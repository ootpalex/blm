Slip on Wet Floor to Sideline Brouillet

December 29, 2057 · From the league's news service

Tim Brouillet will be out of action for the next few weeks after slipping on a wet floor at his home. The Mets third baseman suffered a gluteus strain when his feet went out from underneath him as he unwittingly walked across a recently-mopped kitchen floor. "It's a little embarrassing," said Brouillet of his aching posterior. "I can't even sit down comfortably at the moment." Although teammates have been cracking jokes at his expense, Brouillet is taking the injury in stride. "I'm sure the idea of me falling on my backside amuses them quite a bit, but I'm not laughing about having to be out for 2 weeks. I want to get back on the field and help these guys win some ballgames."
