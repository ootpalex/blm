Gallardo Among Greats in MLB HOF

February 3, 2058 · From the league's news service

A huge crowd watched Rodolfo Gallardo become the newest member of the Major League Baseball Hall of Fame today.

"I'm a big believer in whatever you want, if you want something out of this game, you've got to put the work in it. You've got to work hard," said the outstanding shortstop, whose new plaque described him as "an artisan with a bat whose daily pursuit of excellence produced a .237 lifetime batting average, 2174 hits and 521 home runs."

After the induction ceremony Gallardo was asked what was the most thrilling moment in his career. He responded quickly, "No doubt about it, your first hit in the big leagues. That's tops. It means you're on your way. When you get the first hit, then you can get the rest."
