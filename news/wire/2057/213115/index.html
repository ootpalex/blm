RP Roberto Gutierrez Inks Deal with Detroit

January 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

Sportswriters, fans and team officials watched today as 37-year-old reliever Roberto Gutierrez committed to a $2,200,000, 1-year deal with the Detroit organization.

Some openly worry that despite his talent, Gutierrez is too old to get it done anymore and that his value to the club will sharply diminish before the 1-year agreement has run its course.

On his career ledger Gutierrez has a 62-47 mark, 815 appearances, 123 saves and a 3.47 ERA. He has 1036 strikeouts in 864.2 innings pitched.
