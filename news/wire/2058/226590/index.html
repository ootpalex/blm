Baltimore, Ragsdale Hash out Details of Contract

January 19, 2059 · From the league's news service

Baltimore announced the signing of reliever Dan Ragsdale earlier today, ending speculation on who they would acquire to fill the void at that position.

Ragsdale will earn $2,200,000 per year, and will be with the club for 1 year.

In his career, Ragsdale has 199 saves with a 27-44 record and a 3.69 ERA.
