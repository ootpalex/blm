American League Winner is Bouet

April 8, 2058 · From the league's news service

Not every ball that came off Stéphane Bouet's bat last week was a line drive or a towering shot -- it just seemed that way to the teams that played the Toronto Blue Jays. For his exploits, Bouet was named this week's American League Player of the Week award.

The Toronto third baseman put up 8 hits in 21 at-bats and 3 home runs while hitting .381 with 8 RBIs and 6 runs scored.
