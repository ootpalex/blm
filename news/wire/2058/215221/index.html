Oakland, Cincinnati Execute Trade

March 8, 2058 · From the league's news service

There were plenty of hugs and handshakes all around as 28-year old RHP Cameron Cotter left the Oakland Athletics today en route to joining the Cincinnati Reds organization. 23-year old minor league CF Darien Rice, 19-year old minor league RHP John Moore, and 19-year old minor league RHP Rich Quezada will be traveling to Oakland Athletics to complete the swap.

In his career-to-date, the 28-year-old Cotter has fashioned a 76-33 mark, 3.06 ERA and thrown 994.1 innings. Opposing clubs are batting .216 lifetime against him and he has logged 931 total strikeouts.
