SP Steffes Stays in Boston

May 13, 2058 · From the league's news service

Media analysts hailed Boston's signing of Mike Steffes, one of Major League Baseball's brightest stars, as a pivotal move.

While the deal is certain to raise a few eyebrows, Steffes is expected to earn every bit of the $104,560,000 promised him over 8 years.

On his stats sheet for the year Steffes shows a 6-0 record, 8 starts, 44.2 innings, 43 strikeouts and a 2.22 ERA.
