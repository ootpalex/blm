Nunez Makes 400 HR Mark

May 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

It was a historic day in the life of Edgar Nunez. The 32-year-old Colorado first baseman recorded the 400th homer of his career in the Rockies 3-2 triumph over the Miami Marlins.

In the clubhouse after the game, Nunez told BNN, "I was just looking for a ball to drive, and I got one."

Over his career Nunez is batting .301 with 400 home runs and 1117 RBIs. In 1471 games he has delivered 1710 hits, scored 985 runs, stolen 20 bases and gotten on base at a .374 clip.
