Premier Player Named by American League

November 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

The future looks extremely bright for Sérgio Simard. At the age of 25, many players are still trying to find their way in Major League Baseball.

Simard has not only found his way, he's already established himself as one of the game's brightest stars, today being named the winner of the American League Most Valuable Player Award.

"I didn't expect to hit .368 this year, that's for sure," the first baseman said.

The Minnesota Twins star also put up a .464 on-base percentage this year, with 139 hits, 37 doubles, 1 triple, 36 home runs, 114 RBIs and 86 runs scored.

Marco Castello of the Tampa Bay Rays finished second in voting, while Zach Schuhmann of the Boston Red Sox finished third.
