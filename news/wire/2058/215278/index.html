Ian McCall Signs $6,500,000 Deal with Miami

March 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

Miami and Ian McCall have reached an agreement bringing the 32-year-old starting pitcher to the Marlins for 1 year.

McCall reportedly signed a deal worth $6,500,000, although the financials were not disclosed to the media.

So far in McCall's career, the 32-year-old starter has won 28 and lost 26, collecting 395 strikeouts with a 6.00 ERA. Opponents have hit .283 against him.
