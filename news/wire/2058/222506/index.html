Dominguez Hits for the Cycle

September 25, 2058 · From the league's news service

Gerardo Dominguez made league history at Progressive Field, when he hit for the cycle and added his name to the record books. The Cincinnati Reds took a little gloss off his shine, however, when they grabbed a win, 6-5, versus the Cleveland Guardians.

For the day, Dominguez went 4-for-6 with 2 RBI and 2 runs scored.

"I felt comfortable at the plate in a way I haven't felt all season," Dominguez said after the game. "It was like they were throwing beach balls at me. The ball seemed that big to me. And it just floated in."

Gerardo Dominguez grounded out in the 1st, tripled in the 4th, hit a solo-shot off Danny Ruiz in the 6th, singled in the 8th, grounded out in the 9th and hit an RBI double in the 12th.

Dominguez is currently hitting at a .327 pace with 5 home runs and 27 RBIs.
