Huerta Sizzles with 20-Game Batting Streak

June 25, 2058 · From the league's news service

Miguel Huerta collected 1 hit in today's game and extended his hitting streak to 20 games. His club, the Washington Nationals, beat the San Diego Padres, 5-1.

"Right now, when they throw me the ball, it looks the size of a softball," Huerta quipped to a BNN reporter after the game. "I'm excited about how things are clicking."
Miguel Huerta flied out in the 1st, singled in the 3rd, flied out in the 4th, grounded out in the 5th and struck out in the 8th.

This season, Huerta is batting .316 with 16 home runs. He has scored 56 runs and driven in 50.
