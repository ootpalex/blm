Baltimore First Baseman Out

April 28, 2058 · From the league's news service

Baltimore fans held their breath as Nathan Stienstra left yesterday's Athletics-Orioles contest with an injury. After the game, the team's medical staff said that the 24-year-old first baseman had suffered a strained rib cage muscle while running the bases. It's estimated that he will be out 5-6 weeks.

Thus far this season, Stienstra has hit .230 with 3 home runs, 10 RBIs and scored 9 times.
