Injury Setback for Reds Johnston

May 30, 2058 · From the league's news service

After being examined by team doctors yesterday, Reds starting pitcher Bryce Johnston was told that his rehabilitation time would be increased to 2 weeks because he still had some pain and soreness. The medical staff did not want to activate him until he is totally healthy. Johnston is recovering from back spasms.

This season, Johnston has a 0-3 mark in 5 starts with a 5.68 ERA. He has given up 21 hits in 19 innings, struck out 18 and issued 5 walks. Opposing clubs have compiled a .276 batting average against him.
