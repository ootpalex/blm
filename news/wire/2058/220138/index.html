Rubio Dominates in 8-6 Win over National League All-Stars

July 16, 2058 · From the league's news service

The best players the MLB has to offer took to Dodger Stadium tonight for an all-star showcase.

When the dust settled, the American League bested the National League by a score of 8-6.

Helping the American League to victory was Cesar Rubio, who said it's a memory he'll cherish.

"You can tell your kids and your grandkids some day that you were the MVP of the all-star game, that's a cool moment," he said.

His all-time numbers show Rubio is carrying a .270 career batting average with 107 home runs and 356 RBIs.
