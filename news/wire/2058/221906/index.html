Portillo on Fire, Named Player of the Week

September 9, 2058 · From the league's news service

When it came to hitting the baseball, Gabriel Portillo was in a class by himself in the National League this past week. Nobody did it better.

The outstanding right fielder for San Diego tagged opposing pitchers for a .571 average and was voted the Player of the Week Award. Included in his stats were 12 hits in 21 at-bats, 4 homers, 7 runs scored and 9 RBIs.

So far this year, Portillo is batting .267 with 17 home runs, 57 RBIs and 82 runs scored.
