Infante Fires No-Hitter

March 28, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Arizona Diamondbacks were just hoping for a solid outing from Pedro Infante. Instead, they got something special.

The 28-year-old hurler tossed a no-hitter against the Colorado Rockies, helping the Diamondbacks take the 12-0 win.

"I felt good throughout the game," he said after his spectacular outing for the Diamondbacks. "As I was going into the ninth inning, I wasn't nervous. I knew I had a chance to do something special."

Infante's repertoire has a curveball, fastball, sinker, and changeup, and he used it all to full advantage, keeping the Colorado hitters off balance all game long. The Arizona hurler fanned 12 and walked 2 in his history-making performance.
