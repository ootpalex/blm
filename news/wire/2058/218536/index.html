Garbarino Has Nagging Injury, Listed DTD

June 5, 2058 · From the league's news service

Rays star right fielder Rusty Garbarino might be sitting it out a few days after he picked up an injury in yesterday's game against the Miami Marlins. He sustained back spasms while running the bases. Garbarino isn't expected to miss a lot of time. However, doctors want to be cautious. They said he would be day-to-day for about one week.

In 2058 Garbarino has hit at a .311 clip with 32 hits, 7 home runs, 18 RBIs and has scored 21 times.
