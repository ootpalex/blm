Oakland's Gonzales Will Miss Season

June 25, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Oakland Athletics and their fans were rocked today by the news that star starting pitcher Miguel Gonzales would miss the rest of the season due to injury. Gonzales was hurt in a recent game against the Los Angeles (A) Angels while throwing a pitch. He was immediately removed from the game and taken for examination. Tests today revealed the injury was a torn rotator cuff. His doctors think that Gonzales will miss about 13-14 months.

In the current season in 17 starts, Miguel Gonzales has a record of 9-4 with 88 strikeouts in 105.1 innings and a 2.73 ERA.
