Hit Parade: Shoemaker Pops 5 Hits

May 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

It wasn't just another day at the ballpark for Matt Shoemaker. Shoemaker went on a tear and cracked hit after hit off Pittsburgh pitchers. When the dust settled on the diamond at Oakland Coliseum, he had a 5-for-5 line and a day he will long remember. And the Oakland Athletics had a win, 8-5, over the Pirates.

"Yeah, it'll be something nice to tell the grandkids, but it won't get me a hit tomorrow," he joked in the postgame interview.

Matt Shoemaker singled in the 1st, hit a three-run home run off Adam Bostick in the 3rd, hit an RBI single in the 5th, singled in the 6th and doubled in the 8th.

In 30 games Shoemaker is hitting .289 this season with 35 hits, 2 home runs, 15 RBIs, 13 walks, .356 OBP and has scored 16 runs.
