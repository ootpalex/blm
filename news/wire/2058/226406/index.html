Cincinnati Deals Ressler to Cleveland for Hitchcock

January 9, 2059 · From the league's news service

It's being reported in the Cincinnati press that the Reds have traded 20-year old minor league LF Steve Ressler and 26-year old minor league RHP Josh Presley to the Cleveland Guardians for 26-year old RHP Joe Hitchcock. However, there is no confirmation by the two ball clubs.

The 26-year-old Hitchcock has 220 appearances in his career. He has thrown 247.2 innings with 296 strikeouts and no saves. His won-lost record is 12-13 and his ERA stands at 3.45.
