Garman, Swingle Swap Teams

August 5, 2058 · From the league's news service

In a move that has fans in both cities buzzing, the Baltimore Orioles and the Cincinnati Reds have agreed to a deal that will have 28-year old LHP Chris Garman heading to Cincinnati in exchange for 23-year old minor league 2B Eduardo Aguilar, 19-year old minor league 3B Roy Thomas, and 25-year old minor league RHP Jim Swingle. Provided they pass a physical, the players should be with their respective teams in time for tomorrow's games.

To date Garman has 25 saves with a 3-1 mark and 2.12 ERA. Opposing teams are hitting .183 against him.
