Colorado Won't Make Playoffs

September 21, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Colorado Rockies quite simply didn't have what it took this year. The club was formally eliminated from playoff qualification in 2058.

"It feels bad," said Rockies second baseman Santiago Nuno, when asked for his response by reporters. "Having your biggest hopes of the year dashed feels bad."

The Rockies have made the playoffs 20 times in their 66 years as a Major League Baseball franchise. This playoff miss is the club's 3rd in a row, with their last postseason appearance coming in 2055. In the last 10 seasons, the Rockies have made the playoffs 6 times.
