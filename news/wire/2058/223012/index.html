Tampa Bay Moves on to League Championship Series

October 11, 2058 · From the league's news service

Tampa Bay Rays won a nail-biting series against the Minnesota Twins 3-2, wrapping up the Divison Series finale 16-3 at Target Field.

Rays right fielder Rusty Garbarino was named the series MVP.

He batted .333, drove in 10 runs and scored 4 to lead Tampa Bay into the next round of play.

The Tampa Bay Rays will play against the Boston Red Sox in the next round. Boston won their series against the Kansas City Royals 3 games to 1. The League Championship Series is scheduled to begin on October 12th, 2058..
