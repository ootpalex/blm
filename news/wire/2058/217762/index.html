Miami Trades Aldrich to Cincinnati for Wright

May 13, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Baseball News Network is reporting that the Marlins and Reds have consummated a trade. Cincinnati will receive 32-year old LF Kevin Aldrich in a swap for 23-year old minor league 3B Matt Wright and 21-year old minor league LF Juan Zuniga. A league analyst called the trade a "fairly even transaction."

Aldrich is hitting .273 this year with 7 home runs and 23 RBIs. He also has an OBP of .397, 26 walks and scored 19 times.
