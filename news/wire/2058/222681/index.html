Oakland's Relief Ace Top Pitcher in September

October 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

Andrew Meissner had a super September and has been rewarded with the American League Pitcher of the Month Award. The Oakland relief specialist was very effective, constantly coming in and getting his club out of trouble or protecting a one-run or two-run lead.

the Oakland bullpen ace starred with 7 saves in 12 relief appearances, logging 14 innings, 23 strikeouts, 5 walks and a 1.29 ERA. Meissner finished the month with 4 wins and 1 loss -- and was named the league's top pitcher.

This season, Meissner has crafted a 9-5 won-lost mark, pitched in 66 relief appearances and rung up 41 saves. He has a 3.33 ERA and has allowed 50 hits over his 73 innings of work, struck out 120 batters and walked 31.
