Homer #300 for Washington's Addeo

April 17, 2058 · From the league's news service

The crowd roared -- they knew they were seeing history -- and Cody Addeo was delighted, too. The 36-year-old Nationals third baseman was making history with his 300th career homer. The crowd buzzed and roared again when Addeo stuck his head out of the dugout for a curtain call.

"Never, as a kid, did I ever think I'd hit even one," he told BNN after hitting No. 300 in a 6-3 victory over Los Angeles (N). Addeo went 2-for-3 in the game, had 3 runs batted in, and scored 2 runs.

Over his career Addeo has batted .262 and collected 1536 hits, 300 home runs and 834 RBIs.
