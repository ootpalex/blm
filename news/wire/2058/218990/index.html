Luna Raps Colorado for 5 Hits

June 16, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Pittsburgh Pirates tripped up the Colorado Rockies, 13-12. The Pirates were led by Antonio Luna, their 25-year-old first baseman. Luna made life miserable for Colorado pitchers all day, tagging them for 5 hits in 7 at-bats. Antonio Luna flied out in the 1st, singled in the 3rd, singled in the 5th, doubled in the 7th, hit a GRAND SLAM off Matt Donovan in the 8th, struck out in the 10th and hit an RBI single in the 12th.

On the season, Luna is batting .199 with 10 home runs and 29 RBIs. He has played in 69 games.
