Guardians Johnson Gone for Year

September 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

The doctors have spoken and Chris Johnson of the Cleveland Guardians didn't like what he heard. The 23-year-old reliever is almost certainly gone for the rest of the year and depending on his progress could miss some of next season as well. Johnson sustained his season-ending injury during the Chicago (A) White Sox game yesterday at Guaranteed Rate Field. He had to leave the game because he hurt himself while throwing a pitch. Today the Cleveland medical staff reported Johnson had radial nerve compression and would be absent from the lineup for at least 6 months.

To this point in the season Johnson has 3 saves in 58 relief appearances with a 4-6 record and 3.90 ERA.
