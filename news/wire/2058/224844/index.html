CL Lujan Thrilled with Boston Deal

November 28, 2058 · From the league's news service

A contract extension between Lorenzo Lujan and Boston was reached earlier today.

Lujan will continue to play for the Red Sox and will pocket $16,000,000 over the next 2 years. The Red Sox said the extension "shows our level of confidence in him and in the type of player and person he is."

Lujan has converted 145 saves in his career. Over 394 games he has compiled a 3.79 ERA, 27 wins, 38 losses and 356 strikeouts in 416 innings pitched.
