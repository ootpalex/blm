SP Bostick Lands $27,000,000 Deal with Atlanta

February 18, 2059 · From the league's news service

Braves officials presented Adam Bostick to members of the local print media today as their new starting pitcher.

The deal, worth $27,000,000, guarantees the 34-year-old a home in Atlanta for 2 years. It should improve the ballclub, assuming he plays to his potential.

In career stats, the 34-year-old Bostick has won 69 and lost 69, recording 1146 strikeouts and 342 walks in 1255.1 innings with a 4.04 ERA. Opponents have hit .242 against him.
