3 Roundtrippers for Almaguer

September 8, 2058 · From the league's news service

Robby Almaguer went yard three times in a Philadelphia Phillies victory over the Miami Marlins at loanDepot Park.

"I was in a groove," he said. "Every pitch they tossed up there looked like a grapefruit."

For the day, the Philadelphia shortstop went 3-for-5 against Miami with 4 RBIs and 3 runs scored in a brilliant display of power hitting that led the Phillies to the 8-4 win.

"I tell you what. I'm shocked because I didn't think that last ball had enough to get out," he told BNN reporters in the clubhouse following the game. "I thought it was just going to be a long out."

Robby Almaguer flied out in the 2nd, flied out in the 4th, hit a two-run home run off Josh Queen in the 6th, hit a solo-shot off Ryan Baxter in the 8th and hit a solo-shot off Gary Dougherty in the 9th.

This year, Almaguer is hitting .259 with 20 home runs and 46 RBIs in 69 games played. He also boasts an OBP of .298 with 14 walks and 42 runs scored.
