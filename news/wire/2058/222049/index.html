Jeffries Is King of Swing: Goes Deep 3 Times

September 12, 2058 · From the league's news service

Miami shortstop Jarod Jeffries blasted three homers at Nationals Park in the 7-1 Marlins victory over the Washington Nationals.

For the game, Jarod Jeffries scored 3 times and knocked in 5 runs. When he hit his last home run, his teammates, hearing the crack of the bat, leaped off the bench and rushed on the field to greet the jubilant Jeffries when he reached home plate.

"It was just one game, but it sure was a lot of fun -- 5-for-5, three roundtrippers and a win," a gleeful Jeffries told BNN reporters in a postgame interview. "What a day."

Jarod Jeffries hit an RBI double in the 1st, singled in the 3rd, hit a solo-shot off Juan Velaquez in the 5th, hit a two-run home run off Juan Romero in the 7th and hit a solo-shot off Gene King in the 9th.

This season, Jeffries has banged out 93 hits in 434 at-bats for a .214 average with 19 home runs. He is getting on base at a .302 pace and his totals also include 65 RBIs and 54 runs scored.
