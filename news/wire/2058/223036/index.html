Pirates Heading to League Championship Series

October 12, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Pittsburgh Pirates and Chicago (N) Cubs held nothing back in an entertaining Divison Series that went the distance.

In the end, it will be Pittsburgh advancing to the League Championship Series, after a 12-2 triumph in the final game at PNC Park, to wrap up the series 3-2.

The Pirates players celebrated jubilantly in their clubhouse after the game, while the scene was one of dismay on the opposing side for the Cubs.

Conrado Braves was named series MVP for Pittsburgh. For the series, Braves hit .500 with a .579 on-base percentage. He had 0 home runs, drove in 1 RBI and scored 3 runs.

The Pittsburgh Pirates will play against the Milwaukee Brewers in the next round. Milwaukee won their series against the Los Angeles (N) Dodgers 3 games to 1. The League Championship Series is scheduled to begin on October 12th, 2058.
