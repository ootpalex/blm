Robison Reaches 400-Save Milestone in 3-2 Kansas City Victory

April 19, 2058 · From the league's news service

In an effort that will possibly punch his ticket to the Major League Baseball Hall of Fame, Brandon Robison notched his 400th career save today at Kauffman Stadium, leading the Kansas City Royals to a 3-2 triumph over the Baltimore Orioles.

Known for his calm demeanor in the heat of competition, the 34-year-old Robison was similarly collected after the game.

"To me it's just a number. I wasn't really nervous or thinking too much about it. I'm just trying to make my pitches and get hitters out," he said. The star reliever allowed no hits and no runs, while working 1 innings in recording his milestone save.

Lifetime Robison has saved 400 games with a 44-59 mark and a 2.84 ERA.
