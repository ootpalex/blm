Orlando Ramirez is August's #1 Hitter in NL

September 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

Orlando Ramirez battered National League pitchers all August and wound up taking home the Batter of the Month Award.

"I've been around Major League Baseball long enough to know you really have to work hard day in and day out to be able to put up this kind of consistency," the 29-year-old Ramirez told reporters. "I put in a lot of work, even on the days I might not take the field, and it's always nice when everything comes together for me like it did this last month."

In 27 games, Ramirez hit .327 with 11 home runs, 23 RBIs and scored 22 runs.

This season, Ramirez has banged out 121 hits in 412 at-bats for a .294 average with 27 home runs. He is getting on base at a .402 pace and his totals also include 88 RBIs and 86 runs scored.
