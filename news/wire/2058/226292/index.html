Second Baseman Installed in Hall of Fame

January 9, 2059 · From the league's news service

Chad Partington, the great second baseman and consummate professional, was inducted into the Major League Baseball Hall of Fame today.

At the induction ceremony, he grew misty-eyed as he recalled the highlights of his playing career: his first base hit, his first home run and -- humorously -- the first time he was thrown out at home plate.

Partington says he blew through his third base coach's stop sign. As he returned to the dugout, his manager simply glared at him.

"His eyes just burned a hole right through me," Partington chuckled. "If looks could kill, I wouldn't be here today."

More often than not, Partington pleased his managers and coaches during a superb career.

His all-time numbers show Partington compiled a .291 batting average and collected 3113 hits, 466 home runs and 1544 RBIs. In all, he played in 2730 games. Partington retired from baseball in 2053.
