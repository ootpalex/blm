No Playoff Berth Coming for Texas

September 14, 2058 · From the league's news service

Any small glimmer of hope that the Texas Rangers might have had that they could make a miracle turnaround and win their way into relevancy has been extinguished. The team was mathematically eliminated from playoff contention today.

The Rangers have made the playoffs 18 times in their 98 years as a Major League Baseball franchise. This playoff miss is the club's 3rd in a row, with their last postseason appearance coming in 2055. In the last 10 seasons, the Rangers have only made the playoffs once.
