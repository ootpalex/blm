Lawson Caps Great Week

July 8, 2058 · From the league's news service

Jake Lawson of the Rangers relied on a sharp eye and a quick bat to outpace all other American League hitters last week and collect the Player of the Week Award.

He hit .478 with 11 hits in 23 at-bats, 3 home runs and 11 RBIs.

Looking at his season stats, Lawson has compiled a .249 batting average and a .370 on-base percentage, while hitting 17 home runs and driving in 55 runs.
