Relief Ace Cops #1 Pitcher Award in May

June 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

What Alex Menendez of the Milwaukee Brewers did to National League hitters in May was criminal. He assaulted them, broke them and walked off leaving them lifeless and lost as he stole away with the Pitcher of the Month award for his barbaric behavior.

"Menendez was truly dominant out there," an opposing hitter said to reporters. "He really made hitters miserable."

The 24-year-old reliever threw 21.1 innings in 15 relief appearances as he registered 6 saves, 37 strikeouts, and notched a 3-0 record and 1.27 ERA for the month.

Menendez has 8 saves in 27 relief appearances this season with a 3-0 mark and 1.12 ERA. In 40.1 innings he has 67 strikeouts and 8 walks. Other clubs are hitting .161 against him.
