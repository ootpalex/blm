Great Stroke: Lee Gets 5 Hits

September 17, 2058 · From the league's news service

It seemed that Philadelphia right fielder Brian Lee could do no wrong as the Phillies KO'd the Brewers, 11-6. Lee rapped out 5 hits in 5 at-bats and seemed to know which pitches Brewers hurlers were throwing.

"I think the Phillies were stealing signs," growled one unhappy Milwaukee fan after the game.

"I felt good. I saw the ball well and just wanted to make good contact and put the ball in play," said the jubilant Lee in the postgame press interview.

Brian Lee singled in the 2nd, singled in the 4th, singled in the 6th, doubled in the 7th and hit an RBI double in the 9th.

Thus far this season, Lee has hit .281 with 15 home runs, 59 RBIs and scored 71 times.
