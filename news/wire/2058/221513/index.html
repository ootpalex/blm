New York (A)'s Spangenberg Out 11-12 Months

August 25, 2058 · From the league's news service

The New York (A) Yankees and their fans were rocked today by the news that star closer Tim Spangenberg would miss the rest of the season due to injury. Spangenberg was hurt in a recent game against the Colorado Rockies while throwing a pitch. He was immediately removed from the game and taken for examination. Tests today revealed the injury was a torn labrum. His doctors think that Spangenberg will miss about 11-12 months.

To date this season, Spangenberg has fashioned a 2.15 ERA and rung up 29 saves for his club. He has 64 strikeouts and 10 walks. Opponents have hit for a combined .207 average against him and Spangenberg has a 3-3 won-lost mark.
