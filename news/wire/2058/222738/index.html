Coleman Day-to-Day with Injury

October 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

Chicago (A) team officials speculated whether star starting pitcher Alan Coleman would be scratched from today's ballgame after he suffered a mild abdominal strain in yesterday's matchup with the Tampa Bay Rays. Coleman denied reports that he would miss a lot of time, but added that he would not risk further injury by playing.

Currently Coleman has a 14-7 won-lost record in 33 starts with a 2.34 ERA. He also has 259 strikeouts in 203.2 innings.
