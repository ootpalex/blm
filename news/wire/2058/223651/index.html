NL Hands Out Reliever of the Year

November 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

He may only be 24, but Alex Menendez of the Milwaukee Brewers has already established himself as one of the elite relievers in the National League.

The reliever was today named the NL Reliever of the Year winner for 2058.

He won the award with a strong year that saw him put up 16 saves in 81 relief appearances and 178 strikeouts in 119.2 innings. He had a record of 13-2 to go with his 1.81 ERA.

Michael Hebert of the New York (N) Mets finished second in voting, while David Parra of the Philadelphia Phillies finished third.
