Star of the Week Picked in NL

September 2, 2058 · From the league's news service

It was an extraordinary week for Ryan Adcock of the Los Angeles (N) Dodgers. First he goes on a hot hitting streak, batting .421, then he is voted the Player of the Week in the National League. Last week Adcock went 8-for-19, racked up 3 home runs and 9 RBIs and scored 6 runs. It was a heck of a week for the Los Angeles (N) center fielder.

Currently Adcock is batting .288 with 129 hits and 43 home runs. He has knocked in 93 runs and scored 80 times.
