Royals Prevail, as Wild Card Series Goes to the Wire

October 3, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Kansas City Royals and Oakland Athletics held nothing back in an entertaining Wild Card Series that went the distance.

In the end, it will be Kansas City advancing to the Divison Series, after a 9-7 triumph in the final game at Oakland Coliseum, to wrap up the series 2-1.

The Royals players celebrated jubilantly in their clubhouse after the game, while the scene was one of dismay on the opposing side for the Athletics.

Jeremy Winters was named series MVP for Kansas City. For the series, Winters hit .300 with a .364 on-base percentage. He had 1 home runs, drove in 4 RBI and scored 2 runs.

The Kansas City Royals will play against the Boston Red Sox in the next round. Boston had a bye in the Wild Card Series. The Divison Series is scheduled to begin on October 4th, 2058.
