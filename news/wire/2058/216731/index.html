Another Setback for Miami's Nolan Lee

April 20, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Miami Marlins announced today that center fielder Nolan Lee will not be returning as quickly as expected from injury. "There was a bit of a setback, but nothing to be concerned about," one team official told reporters. "Nolan will probably miss another one week because of this, but everyone expects him to make a full recovery."

Currently Lee has no home runs and a .261 batting average.
