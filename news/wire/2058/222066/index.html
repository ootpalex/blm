Title Captured - American League Central Division Won by Minnesota

September 14, 2058 · From the league's news service

Members of the Minnesota Twins had to fight back tears as they celebrated an American League Central Division championship tonight.

"It means so much to a lot of the guys in this room, especially the veterans," said starting pitcher Tom Nowak. "There are guys on this team that know how to win and what it takes and others that are just figuring it out. It's exciting for all of us."

The Twins boast a record of 100-47, and the division title is the 36th in team history.

Minnesota will be going for its 6th Major League Baseball championship.
