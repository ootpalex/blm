Baltimore-Philadelphia Announce Swap

August 5, 2058 · From the league's news service

The Baseball News Network is reporting that the Orioles and Phillies have consummated a trade. Philadelphia will receive 31-year old 2B Jason Phelps and $1,000,000 in cash in a swap for 21-year old minor league 2B Tim Gibson and 24-year old minor league LHP Doug Hall. A league analyst called the trade a "fairly even transaction."

Baltimore will retain a portion of Phelps's remaining contract.

Hitting .290 to date, Phelps has 5 home runs and 37 RBIs. He has played in 105 games.
