New York (N) RP Cody Benson Injured

September 17, 2058 · From the league's news service

When Cody Benson was hurt against the Washington Nationals, the New York (N) Mets were hopeful that the injury was not serious. However, it proved to be more severe than first thought. The 29-year-old reliever suffered a torn rotator cuff throwing a pitch in yesterday's game. Team doctors told BNN today that Benson would miss 6 months of the season.

To date this year, Benson has won 5 and lost 4 with a 5.79 ERA, 1 save and 70 strikeouts in 58 relief appearances.
