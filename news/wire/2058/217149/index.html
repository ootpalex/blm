Tinoco Picks up NL Monthly Pitching Honor

May 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

Aaron Tinoco had an excellent April and has been rewarded with the National League Pitcher of the Month Award. The Pittsburgh relief ace was one tough customer on the mound, constantly coming in and getting his club out of trouble or protecting a one-run or two-run lead.

During the month, the Pirates star had 4 saves in 5 save opportunities, a 0.00 ERA and 4 wins and no losses. In 9.2 innings Tinoco struck out 11 and gave up 5 hits and 5 walks.
