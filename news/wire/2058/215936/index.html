Titterington Put on Shelf, Listed DTD

April 1, 2058 · From the league's news service

Ewan Titterington of the Pittsburgh Pirates was hurt in yesterday's game with the Nationals. He sustained a sore back while throwing the ball and will be listed day-to-day. Team doctors said it could be one week before the standout shortstop will be completely healed. He could miss a few games.

So far this season, Titterington is batting .353 with 2 home runs, 3 RBIs and 3 runs scored. He has 6 hits in 17 at-bats, 2 walks and a .421 OBP. Titterington has played in 5 games.
