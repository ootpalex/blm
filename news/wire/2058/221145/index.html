Robinson Wins NL Player of the Week Prize

August 12, 2058 · From the league's news service

This week Chance Robinson, left fielder for the Cincinnati Reds, slashed, mashed and bashed his way to the National League Player of the Week trophy.

Robinson put up a .407 average as he pounded out 11 hits, 3 home runs, 6 RBIs and scored 5 runs.

This year, Robinson has hit at a .287 clip with 84 hits, 13 home runs, 40 RBIs and 39 runs scored.
