Boston, Vasquez Hash out Details of Contract

February 2, 2059 · From the league's news service

Our story Red Sox Bet One Guaranteed Year on Jorge Vasquez's October Résumé

The Boston front office announced the signing of Jorge Vasquez today as the team's new starting pitcher. The 37-year-old came to terms with the Red Sox and will be hauling in $38,533,333 a year over 3 years.

Over his career Vasquez has 205 victories and 123 defeats with a 3.78 ERA. The 37-year-old hurler also has struck out 3014 batters in 3018.1 innings and surrendered 2672 hits.
