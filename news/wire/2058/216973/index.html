Los Angeles (N) Loses Angel Moya

April 25, 2058 · From the league's news service

Earlier today, Los Angeles (N) left fielder Angel Moya had to leave the game against Washington because of an injury. Team officials are reporting that he has a fractured foot and will miss 3 weeks of play. Moya was hurt being hit by a pitch and was unable to continue playing. He will be evaluated this afternoon by team physicians.

Moya is currently batting .297 with 2 home runs, 14 runs scored and 6 RBIs. His OBP is .379.
