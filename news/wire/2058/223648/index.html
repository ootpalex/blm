American League Names Silver Slugger Award Winners

November 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

For their standout offensive performances, the American League today announced the winners of the Silver Slugger Award. The winners for 2058 are:

Catcher
Justin Strasbaugh (Texas Rangers)
.266/.342/.499, 459 AB, 24 HR, 1 SB, 1.0 WAR

First Baseman
Sérgio Simard (Minnesota Twins)
.368/.464/.757, 378 AB, 36 HR, 16 SB, 114 RBI, 7.6 WAR

Second Baseman
David Saldivar (Seattle Mariners)
.297/.383/.486, 580 AB, 22 HR, 20 SB, 6.2 WAR

Third Baseman
Brian Roberti (Chicago (A) White Sox)
.294/.365/.511, 487 AB, 22 HR, 5 SB, 4.4 WAR

Shortstop
Pat Hagen (Minnesota Twins)
.280/.333/.498, 482 AB, 27 HR, 5 SB, 4.8 WAR

Left Fielder
Zach Schuhmann (Boston Red Sox)
.331/.375/.582, 619 AB, 37 HR, 104 RBI, 6.3 WAR

Center Fielder
Lorenzo Santi (Minnesota Twins)
.293/.353/.489, 597 AB, 21 HR, 32 SB, 103 R, 6.3 WAR

Right Fielder
Coby Houghton (Chicago (A) White Sox)
.287/.321/.524, 513 AB, 31 HR, 2 SB, 3.6 WAR

Designated Hitter
John Stuckey (Minnesota Twins)
.308/.371/.522, 601 AB, 22 HR, 45 SB, 125 R, 5.7 WAR
