Roberti Chosen #1 Player

September 9, 2058 · From the league's news service

What's in a number? In baseball, just about everything.

The numbers that Chicago (A)'s Brian Roberti put up last week were the envy of the league, as Roberti came away with American League Player of the Week honors.

He belted out 8 hits in 14 at-bats, 2 home runs and 7 RBIs last week as he posted his .571 batting average.

This season, Roberti is hitting .294 with 22 home runs.
