Reds Pitching Ravaged by Garcia

July 21, 2058 · From the league's news service

Gus Garcia put on a hitting clinic at Nationals Park, spraying hits all over the ballpark and lashing Cincinnati pitchers for 5 hits in 7 at-bats. However, the offensive onslaught wasn't enough for the Washington Nationals, who still fell, 12-10.

"Sometimes I try to pull the ball too much, so today I just went with the pitch," Garcia told reporters after the game. "It's great to get 5 hits, but it would have been nicer to win the ballgame. too."

Gus Garcia singled in the 1st, hit a two-run home run off Bryce Johnston in the 3rd, singled in the 5th, struck out in the 7th, singled in the 9th, grounded out in the 11th and hit a two-run home run off Jesse Carr in the 13th.

Garcia is hitting .255 this year with 14 home runs, 38 RBIs and 35 runs scored. He has 56 hits in 220 at-bats, 30 walks and a .354 on-base percentage. Garcia has played in 58 games.
