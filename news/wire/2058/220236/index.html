Houston-Seattle Fight Results in Suspensions

July 20, 2058 · From the league's news service

Yesterday at T-Mobile Park Mike Baily and Kevon Gibbs got into each other's space and then into each other's face. They began to grapple and fell to the ground. That's when it boiled over into a full-fledged bench-clearing tussle between the Houston Astros and Seattle Mariners. Reason finally prevailed as the umpires restored order. After much discussion, only the two major combatants were ejected from the game.

Today the league office notified the ballclubs that Baily will have to sit out 5 while Gibbs would be given a 6-game suspension for their roles in the brawl.

For the year, Baily has hit at a .226 clip with 71 RBIs, 29 home runs and 53 runs scored.

This year, Gibbs has worked 114.2 innings with 90 strikeouts en route to a 7-7 record and a 3.69 ERA.
