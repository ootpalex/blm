National League Top Rookie Named

November 10, 2058 · From the league's news service

Ryan Adcock, first baseman for the Los Angeles (N) Dodgers, had a good freshman year, highlighted today by his selection for the National League Rookie of the Year Award for 2058.

The standout Dodgers first baseman took the honor by hitting .299 in 148 games this season and finishing with 160 hits, 51 home runs, 118 RBIs and 103 runs scored.

Daniel Johnson of the Milwaukee Brewers finished second in voting, while Miguel Davila of the Los Angeles (N) Dodgers finished third.
