Big Bang! 3 HR for New York (A)'s Stoppard

September 21, 2058 · From the league's news service

It was a good day for the New York (A) Yankees and an exceptional one for Harvey Stoppard. The 24-year-old third baseman sent three bombs out of the park to help the Yankees beat the Oakland Athletics, 6-3, at Oakland Coliseum.

"When you're in the zone, the game really slows down," Stoppard said when asked about his performance later. "I couldn't even tell you what pitches were thrown. Today feels great, but it is a long season and tomorrow is another day."

For the day, he scored 3 times, drove in 5 runs and smacked 3 hits in 3 at-bats.

Harvey Stoppard hit a solo-shot off Alex Mireles in the 2nd, hit a three-run home run off Alex Mireles in the 3rd, hit a solo-shot off Chris Moore in the 6th and walked in the 8th.

In 16 games Stoppard is hitting .255 this season with 14 hits, 9 home runs, 19 RBIs, 7 walks, .354 OBP and has scored 10 runs.
