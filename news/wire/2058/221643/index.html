Rodencal on Fire, 5-for-6 Against Detroit

August 30, 2058 · From the league's news service

Ian Rodencal exploded for a barrage of base hits at Comerica Park.

The Boston shortstop had a field day in his team's 10-1 triumph over the Detroit Tigers, finishing 5-for-6.

"My hitting philosophy is simple," he said. "I focus on just seeing the ball and I try to get a good pitch."

Rodencal must have focused at least 5 times and apparently got some good pitches, too, leading to one of his best days ever with the bat. Ian Rodencal singled in the 1st, doubled in the 3rd, doubled in the 5th, struck out in the 7th, hit a two-run single in the 8th and hit an RBI single in the 9th.

This year, Rodencal has batted at a .251 pace with 95 hits, 13 home runs, 50 RBIs and scored 65 times.
