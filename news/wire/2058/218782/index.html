League Rules Pena Out 2 Games

June 12, 2058 · From the league's news service

Willie Pena was suspended by the league for 2 games for "inappropriate conduct on the field," stemming from an altercation during a game with the Boston Red Sox yesterday at Fenway Park. The Philadelphia catcher refused to leave the field for several minutes after being ejected by the home plate umpire over a third-strike call. His manager finally convinced him to leave the field, but had to step between him and the umpire several times.

Phillies officials told the press Pena was not available for comment after the game.

This season, Pena is hitting .277 with 18 home runs.
