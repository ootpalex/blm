Blue Jays Swap Rodriguez to Rays for Gomez

June 3, 2058 · From the league's news service

After prolonged negotiations Toronto agreed to ship 24-year old C Jose Rodriguez to Tampa Bay for 26-year old 3B Montes Gomez. The deal is awaiting confirmation from the league office but neither team expects any hang-ups.

This season, Rodriguez is batting .222 with 12 hits and 1 home run. He has scored 4 times and has 7 runs batted in.

This season, Gomez is hitting .330 with 29 hits, 3 home runs, 11 RBIs, scored 12 runs and played in 23 games.
