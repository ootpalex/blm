Season Ends for Randy Elam of Los Angeles (N)

February 19, 2059 · From the league's news service

Doctors in Los Angeles (N) said Randy Elam might not play anymore this year. The Dodgers starting pitcher suffered a torn ulnar collateral ligament during a game against the San Diego Padres and will be out for at least 10 months. Club officials told BNN they do not expect him to return to the team this season.
