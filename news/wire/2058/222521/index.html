Chicago (A) Makes the Playoffs

September 26, 2058 · From the league's news service

For the Chicago (A) White Sox, it's not how you start, but how you finish. That's the mantra the team is following after today qualifying for the playoffs.

"We may be going into the playoffs as a wild card team, but we plan to finish the playoffs as champions," said closer Dan Patterson. "There's no shortage of confidence from guys in our clubhouse."

With a record of 91-67, there will of course be teams with better track records in the postseason.
