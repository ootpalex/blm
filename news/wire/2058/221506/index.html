Paramo Razzle-Dazzles with No-Hitter

August 24, 2058 · From the league's news service

Oakland is abuzz in the wake of a masterful no-hitter by Alex Paramo.

The 25-year-old hurler was dominating against the Milwaukee Brewers, leading the Athletics to a 3-0 win at Oakland Coliseum.

Paramo had 11 strikeouts and gave up 1 walk en route to the no-hitter.

"Yeah, this was a special day for me," Paramo said. "I had my good stuff and good movement. Good location, too. Even when I made a mistake, I got away with it today."

This season, Paramo has 12 wins and 11 losses and a 2.83 ERA.
