Pirates Lose Jackson for Season

March 6, 2059 · From the league's news service

The news is bad in Pittsburgh. The Pirates have lost the services of starting pitcher Alpha Jackson for the rest of the season due to a torn ulnar collateral ligament. Jackson was injured in a recent game with the St. Louis Cardinals while throwing a pitch and is expected to miss 13 months.
